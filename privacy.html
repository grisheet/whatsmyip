Privacy

GitHub hosts this page and may log requests for it under its privacy policy. The application does not set cookies or use analytics.

Lookup requests

When you press “Look up”, your browser sends the address you entered to ipapi.is over HTTPS. When you press “Use my IP”, ipapi.is uses your connection address for the lookup. ipapi.is receives your connection address for both actions and handles requests under its own policies. Opening this page alone sends no request to that provider.

Successful results are reusable in this page for five minutes. The site does not save lookup history or persist the cache after you reload or close the page.
